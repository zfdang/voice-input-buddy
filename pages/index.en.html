HOW IT WORKS

A bridge between your hardware
and your input tool.

①

Your remote

Microphone audio + buttons

→
②

Voice Input Buddy

Decode audio · Trigger shortcuts

→
③

Input method / dictation tool

Recognize speech · Enter text

Your remote sends audio over Bluetooth. Voice Input Buddy decodes it, routes it into a virtual microphone, and triggers the voice shortcut. Recognition, live text, and corrections come from the input tool; Buddy does not run a speech-recognition model.

GET STARTED

One installer.
Four steps to get ready.

The app and microphone driver install together.
No need for two separate downloads.

Setup and troubleshooting ↗
  1. 01

    Install and open

    Download the PKG from Releases and open Voice Input Buddy in Applications.

  2. 02

    Check the environment

    Follow the guide for Bluetooth, Input Monitoring, and Accessibility permissions.

  3. 03

    Connect and configure

    Connect your remote and test its buttons. Once the driver check passes, click Continue. The app automatically uses Voice Input Buddy Mic; select that microphone in your input tool and configure its voice shortcut.

  4. 04

    Focus a text field. Speak.

    Complete your tool’s setup guide and voice test, then hold the remote voice button to speak and release to finish. For tap-based tools such as NetEase Bageshuo and Typeless, enable “Use Fn Taps for Voice Typing” so the app sends start and finish shortcuts around audio capture.

TROUBLESHOOTING

Voice works, but buttons do not?

Check device access in Karabiner-Elements and other key remappers.

A remapper can take exclusive access to the remote, preventing Buddy from receiving its buttons. Ordinary buttons may stop performing their assigned actions while Bluetooth or voice input still works. By default, Home switches apps, TV plays or pauses media, Power sends Backspace, and Back sends Escape.

  1. Open Devices in Karabiner-Elements and find your remote, such as the Xiaomi Bluetooth Voice Remote.
  2. Turn off Modify events for that remote so Buddy can handle it. Keep your other keyboard settings.
  3. Reconnect the remote or restart Buddy, check its status in Button Mapping, and test the home, TV, power, and back buttons.

If Buddy still reports that another input tool owns the device, restart Karabiner-Elements and try again. Also check Buddy’s Input Monitoring and Accessibility permissions.

LOCAL BRIDGE. CLEAR BOUNDARIES.

No recording archive. No transcript history.

Buddy does not collect usage statistics or upload logs. Check your input tool’s settings for its own network and recognition behavior.

Installation requires administrator authorization. A first install or driver change briefly restarts audio. The current PKG is unsigned and not notarized by Apple, so macOS may block installation.
